One image summing up the world news of 27 November 2026, drawn as a
newspaper front page. `main.py` (standard library only) copies the
front-page image `front.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.

## Stories covered

- **Lead — Drones kill 2 Italians in Kuwait:** two Rheinmetall
  technicians die at a Kuwaiti air-defence station; Kuwait and Italy
  blame Iranian drones, Iran denies any role (Reuters / BBC, 26 Nov).
- **Israel kills 10 in Gaza:** strikes hit a Hamas official and his
  wife in Deir al-Balah on a third night of attacks; Hamas says it
  will consult allies on a response (Reuters / AP, 26 Nov).
- **Russia hits Kyiv with ballistic missiles:** a shopping centre is
  struck, three people are killed and power is cut in seven regions
  (Reuters, 26 Nov).
- **Poland asks NATO to down Russian missiles:** Warsaw wants allies
  to shoot down missiles over Ukraine after three Kh-101s crossed
  into Poland on 20 November; NATO sends an AWACS (Bloomberg, 26 Nov).
- **US proposes new tariffs on 14 partners:** Washington targets
  what it calls excess factory capacity, with the highest rates aimed
  at China (Reuters, 25 Nov).
- **Also today:** Gukesh and Sindarov draw Game 3 of the world chess
  title match; UK net migration falls to its lowest since 1997; a
  tanker blast is reported near Dubai; Trump asks Putin to pull back
  in Europe.

All headlines were checked against reputable wire reporting of
26–27 November 2026 before drawing. The image is 1024×1024 and the
program is deterministic: every run writes the same bytes.
